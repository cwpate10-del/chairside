The short version

Don't do
these things.

Most ruined tattoos aren't ruined by the artist. They're ruined in week one.

  • ✕

    Don't pick or scratch the peeling. You'll pull pigment out with the scab and leave patchy spots that need a touch-up.

  • ✕

    Don't soak it. No pools, lakes, hot tubs, or long baths for two to three weeks. Showers are fine.

  • ✕

    Don't smother it. A thick greasy layer traps bacteria and pulls ink. Thin enough that it doesn't look shiny.

  • ✕

    Don't use scented anything. Fragrance and alcohol sting fresh ink and dry the skin out.

  • ✕

    Don't put it in the sun. Not while healing, not without SPF after. Sun is the number one reason old tattoos look muddy.

  • ✕

    Don't guess if something looks wrong. Spreading redness, heat, or pus means call your artist, then a doctor.
